Không có backpressure

Producer 10k/s -> Queue -> Consumer 2k/s
                     backlog +8k/s -> memory/lag grows

Chiến lược

  • block producer
  • bounded queue
  • drop theo policy
  • rate limit producer
  • scale consumer
  • batch work

Go channel

jobs := make(chan Job, 1000)
jobs <- job // blocks when full
